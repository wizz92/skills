# PDP scoring rubric

Use this rubric for every scored review. Score only the plan and its documented execution.

## Common anchors

- **5 — strong:** complete, specific, current evidence; no material gap for the criterion.
- **4 — good:** the criterion works in practice, with one limited gap.
- **3 — workable:** the foundation exists, but one material gap reduces clarity or execution.
- **2 — weak:** evidence is partial, mostly implicit, stale, or inconsistent; several material gaps exist.
- **1 — absent or unusable:** the essential element is missing, contradictory, or cannot guide action.
- **Н/Д — not assessable:** the review timing or source makes a fair judgment impossible. Explain why.

Use intermediate judgment consistently: `2` and `4` must sit between the neighboring anchors, not substitute for uncertainty. Record uncertainty separately as evidence confidence.

## Criteria and weights

### 1. Development goal and intended outcome — 20%

Check whether:

- the plan states a specific capability or behavior to develop;
- the goal is connected to the person's current role, agreed career direction, or documented business need;
- the intended outcome is observable rather than phrased only as an activity;
- the goal and time horizon are still relevant.

A list of courses without a stated development outcome cannot score above `2`. A clear outcome with a minor measurement gap is normally `4`, not `5`.

### 2. Action quality and success measures — 25%

Check whether:

- actions are concrete enough to execute;
- each major action has an expected result or completion criterion;
- actions include application in real work, practice, or demonstrated output where appropriate;
- milestones and measures allow progress to be verified.

Attendance, reading, or course completion alone proves activity, not development impact. A plan made only of such activities cannot score above `3` here unless it also defines application and evidence.

### 3. Feasibility, ownership, and support — 15%

Check whether:

- major actions have an owner or a clear responsible person;
- dates and sequence are realistic for the plan period;
- dependencies, manager support, mentoring, budget, access, or other needed resources are identified when material;
- the number and difficulty of actions are achievable without making the plan trivial.

Do not assume missing support exists outside the source. State it as missing evidence when the plan could still be feasible without it; lower the score when the missing dependency makes execution unclear.

### 4. Progress, results, and learning evidence — 25%

Check whether:

- actions due by the review date have progressed or have a documented explanation;
- completed actions have evidence of the promised result when the source supports such evidence;
- learning has been applied, demonstrated, or reflected on where appropriate;
- progress since the previous review is meaningful and consistent with planned milestones.

Do not compare displayed progress with elapsed calendar time as if progress were linear. Judge due milestones and actual outcomes. If the plan has just started and nothing is due, use `Н/Д` unless another execution signal exists.

### 5. Maintenance discipline and deadline health — 15%

Check whether:

- statuses and completion states are current;
- due dates are present and internally consistent;
- overdue items have an updated plan, explanation, or recovery action;
- blockers, changes, and next steps are documented when they affect delivery.

Separate an overdue action from an expired plan. A late item with a credible, current recovery plan is healthier than an unexplained stale item, but lateness remains visible evidence.

## Overall score

Calculate the weighted score:

`overall = goal × 0.20 + actions × 0.25 + feasibility × 0.15 + execution × 0.25 + maintenance × 0.15`

Round the result to one decimal place.

If a criterion is `Н/Д`, remove its weight and proportionally normalize the remaining weights. Mark the overall score as `предварительная оценка`. If fewer than three criteria are assessable, do not calculate an overall score.

Health status:

- **Green:** `4.0–5.0` — healthy plan with no major correction needed.
- **Yellow:** `3.0–3.9` — workable plan with material improvements required.
- **Red:** `1.0–2.9` — plan is not reliably guiding development or execution.

The color describes plan health only. List critical facts separately even when the average remains green: no action items, expired plan with unfinished required actions, status/date conflict, or no evidence for a claimed completed outcome.

## Trend

Compare with the latest review that used the same rubric:

- **Improving:** overall score increased by at least `0.3`, or important execution evidence improved without a comparable score change.
- **Stable:** score changed by less than `0.3` and no material risk changed.
- **Declining:** overall score decreased by at least `0.3`, or a material new overdue item, blocker, or status conflict appeared.
- **Baseline:** no comparable prior review exists.

Explain the trend with concrete changes. Never infer causation from the score alone.

## Evidence confidence

- **High:** all five criteria are assessable from current, internally consistent evidence.
- **Medium:** one criterion is `Н/Д`, or some supporting evidence is incomplete but the main conclusion is stable.
- **Low:** two or more criteria are `Н/Д`, the source is stale, or key fields conflict.

Confidence describes evidence coverage, not plan quality.
