# Rating calibration

Use this reference for disputed ratings and levels 4–5.

- Level 4 requires evidence that expectations were met and that the object produced a meaningful improvement.
- Level 5 additionally requires repeated overachievement or measurable wider impact.
- If a high rating is claimed but not supported, report the claim separately and assign the highest level supported by evidence. Do not apply an arbitrary one-level penalty.
- Lack of evidence is a calibration gap, not proof of poor performance.
- Do not reward visible effort when the required result is absent.
- Do not call rating differences bias without suitable evidence; describe them as calibration or rating-pattern signals.
- For legal, safety, financial, medical, academic, or company-controlled decisions, use the mandatory rubric and map it to this scale only when requested.

Explain any cap caused by a failed critical requirement.
